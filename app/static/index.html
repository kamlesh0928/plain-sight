      How PlainSight Works

      1
      1. Understand

      Identifies the apparent document or communication type (SMS, bill, UPI request) in everyday words.

      2
      2. Examine

      Pinpoints observable warning signs such as artificial panic, lookalike sender IDs, and unverified links.

      3
      3. Verify

      Guides you with safe, practical next steps and reviewed official verification hotlines.

      Privacy & Data Guarantee

      • Images are processed in transient server memory and relayed to Google Gemini for visual reasoning.
      • No images or documents are ever written to a database or stored on disk.
      • Sensitive credentials (OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords) are never requested, recorded, or echoed.
